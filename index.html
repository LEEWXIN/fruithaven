About FruitHaven

We source premium fruits directly from trusted farms across Malaysia and beyond — cutting out the middleman so you get fresher produce at honest prices.

🌱

Farm to Door

Every fruit is sourced directly from certified farms. We visit our suppliers regularly to ensure consistent quality and ethical growing practices.

❄️

Cold-Chain Delivery

Our refrigerated logistics network maintains optimal temperatures from farm to your doorstep, preserving freshness and nutritional value.

🔬

Nutrition-First

Each product listing includes detailed nutritional information and health recommendations — because knowing what you eat matters.

🌍

Sustainable Practices

We use recyclable packaging and partner only with farms that follow sustainable agriculture standards, minimising our environmental footprint.

📦

Flexible Orders

Order by piece, kilogram, or box — whatever works for you. We accommodate households of all sizes with no minimum order requirement.

🤝

Community First

A portion of every sale supports local smallholder farmers. Buying from FruitHaven means you're directly contributing to local agricultural communities.
